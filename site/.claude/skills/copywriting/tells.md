# Tells

A tell is a line any site could publish. Hunt for each class below. A word used in its literal sense is fine: "robust oak sleepers", "the journey from London to Zurich", "raised beds elevate the soil".

| Tell | Looks like | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generic promise | "Plan your future with confidence", "Protect what matters most", "Your trusted local experts" | The specific promise with its number: "How much will your pension pay at 67?" |
| Stock word | delve, elevate, seamless, unlock, empower, intuitive, innovative, world-class, cutting-edge, tapestry, testament, bustling, nestled, curated | The plain word, or the fact it stands for |
| Contrast frame | "Not just X, it's Y", "more than just", "by developers, for developers", "where X meets Y" | Say Y |
| Audience list | "Whether you're a beginner or a pro", "from all walks of life" | Write to the one reader in `intent.md` |
| Scene-setting | "In today's fast-paced world", "Imagine a world where", "Navigating X can be overwhelming" | Open with the claim |
| Triad | "Fast, reliable, and affordable", "learn, connect, and grow" | Keep the one that is true and prove it |
| Fragment drumbeat | "No jargon. No hidden fees. No surprises.", "Ship faster. Build smarter." | One sentence with the proof |
| About-us filler | "Welcome to", "We're passionate about", "Our mission is", "What started as a dream" | What you do, for whom, since when |
| Empty CTA | "Get started", "Learn more", "Secure your spot today", "Ready to…?" | The action and its object: "Book a table for Friday" |
| Throat-clearing | "Please note", "It's worth noting", "That's a great question", "That said" | Start with the point |
| Intensifier | truly, incredibly, absolutely, simply, very | Cut it, or prove the claim |
| Chirpy error | "Oops, something went wrong", "Looks like you haven't added anything yet" | What happened and what to do next |
| Polite void | "Thank you for reaching out. A member of our team will get back to you." | Who does what by when: "Ngozi replies by 5pm tomorrow." |
| Title Case | "Where Every Meal Tells a Story" | Sentence case |
| Decoration | Emoji, exclamation marks, dashes for drama | Let the sentence carry it |
