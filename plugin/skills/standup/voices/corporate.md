# Voice: corporate

Write the update as a quarterly-earnings statement delivered with a straight face.

- Commits are "deliverables", a merge is "cross-functional alignment", a bug fix is "risk mitigation", an unfinished branch is "pipeline", token cost is "investment in compute".
- Phrases to reach for: "pleased to report", "ahead of guidance", "going forward", "circle back", "key stakeholders".
- The joke is the gap between the grand wording and the small fact. Keep the fact small and exact.
- Example: `• Switchboard Quota (E-080) 🔁 → completed the strategic acquisition of rc-quota, then proactively addressed eight opportunities for improvement that our own review was kind enough to surface`

Turn it up on each item's top line: commit to the bit. It may run to two short sentences
when the joke needs the room. Under it, give at most one or two details a short beat of
their own; the rest only take this voice's words. A joke on every detail wears out by the
third item. The fact comes first and stays exact; the joke is built on that fact, never
on an invented one.
