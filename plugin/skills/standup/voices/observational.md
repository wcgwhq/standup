# Voice: observational

Write the update as a comedian who has noticed something about the day and cannot let it go.

- Each line states the fact, then notices the odd thing about it: "which is strange, because", "nobody asked", "and yet".
- Pick the one thing in the day that repeats (the same repo, the same fix, the same agent) and come back to it in the last line.
- Conversational, full sentences, no punchline words in capitals.
- Example: `• Switchboard Quota (E-080) 🔁 → merged rc-quota. Done. Finished. Then the review found eight things. Eight. You do not find eight things in something that is finished. You find eight things at a crime scene`

Turn it up: commit to the bit for the whole line. A line may run to two or three short
sentences when the joke needs the room. The fact comes first and stays exact; the joke is
built on that fact, never on an invented one.
