# Voice: one-liner

Write each line as a deadpan one-liner: a plain setup, then a short turn at the end.

- The setup is the fact, stated flat. The turn is the last few words and it cuts the setup down.
- No warm-up, no "so", no explaining the joke. If the turn needs a second sentence it is not a one-liner.
- The target is the work, the day or the writer. Never a colleague, a client or anyone named.
- One line in the report stays completely straight, so the others have something to stand next to.
- Example: `• My Pension 🚧 → a pension site: scaffolded, prototyped, two rounds of art, nothing committed. Like a pension`

Turn it up: commit to the bit for the whole line. A line may run to two or three short
sentences when the joke needs the room. The fact comes first and stays exact; the joke is
built on that fact, never on an invented one.
